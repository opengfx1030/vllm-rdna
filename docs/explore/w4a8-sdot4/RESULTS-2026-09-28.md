Measured on the V620 with ROCm 7.14 (clang 23) by commits a1193e8, 1d601a9
and 93caa86 on this branch. The numbers below are the ones those commit
messages report; the JSON records were not committed, so the per-cell G3
tables stay blank in [TESTPLAN.md](TESTPLAN.md) until they are. The
harness review that follows the numbers was done agent-side on the pushed
code.

## Review of the run

- **G2 never checked a production-shaped cell.** The 12 skips are whole
  cells: 128×6144×2560, 624×6144×2560 and 2048×2560×8704, each for G 32 and
  128 and both formats. The f32 check runs at split 1, which needs
  `M_TILE·K` bytes of LDS: over 64 KiB for every LDS config at K=8704, and
  for `a32n2_lds_k32` at K=2560. The first config that could not launch
  aborted the cell and dropped every other config's result with it. The
  180 passes are the five small EDGE cells. **Fixed here**:
    - a config that cannot run split 1 is checked through the fp16 path
      only, and its f32 column reads n/a;
    - a config is skipped only when no group-aligned split fits, which in
      the sweep means `a32n2_lds_k32` at G=128 with K ≥ 8704;
    - skips no longer count as failures.
- **G3 had the same exposure.** `--split-k 1` or `--group-size 128` would
  have aborted on the first config that does not fit. G3 now validates each
  launch once and records a skip instead.
- **The `pick_split_k` clamp in a1193e8 cannot fire.** The two loops before
  it only stop on a split within the soft budget, which is at most 64 KiB,
  or on the last split. An exhaustive sweep of 20,000+ (M, N, K, G, config)
  cases found no split it changes. The over-cap cases are the ones where
  even the largest group-aligned split is too big, which the skip handles.
  The clamp is harmless; its comment overstates it.
- **The G3 speedups exceed the VALU ceiling.** Even the best per-token
  config models at 1.53× at G=32, so 1.75–2.9× means the W4A16 op is not
  VALU-bound on those cells, or W4A8 wins elsewhere: int8 A halves the LDS
  and A traffic, and the split differs. The commit does not say which
  configs, which cells, or whether it is `× GEMM` or `× total`. G1 rules
  out the per-token configs, so the number that decides is the best `_ag`
  config's `× total`.
- **rel-L2 ≈ 2.5e-2 against the W4A16 op is what a correct kernel gives.**
  The W4A16 op carries its baked fp16 bias of about 2–3 %
  ([PRIOR-ART-RDNA3.md](PRIOR-ART-RDNA3.md) §1), and per-token A8 adds about
  0.8 %.

## What the results change

1. **Per-group activation scales are the main line.** Per-token W4A8 fails
   G1 by 5×, and clipping does not rescue it. Shape (1d) moves to the top,
   and the per-token configs remain only as timing references.
2. **The flush budget is now the per-group one.** The ceilings are 1.36–1.39×
   at G=32 and 1.81–1.86× at G=128 (ISA, clang 18), not 1.47–1.53×.
3. **Split 1 at large K needs a config without LDS staging**, for
   determinism and JartX's no-split rule. At K=8704 no LDS config can run
   split 1 at all.
