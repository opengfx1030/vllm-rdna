# Mono + W4A8: the RDNA2 MonoKernel next to the W4A8 MoE prefill (2026-11-05)

Question: can `--monokernel` run with `VLLM_RDNA2_W4A8_SDOT4=1` (the
`flashnext-mtp2` recipe), and does mono + W4A8 beat production on decode
without losing the W4A8 prefill win or any quality?

## Verdict

**Yes, both run together. Prefill and quality match production within noise.
Decode is up to 3 % faster than the shipped recipe, but only because `--monokernel`
raises the HIP queue budget to 4: production at `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=4` decodes
faster than mono at 4 queues, and mono at 2 queues is slower than production
at 2.**

- **It boots and serves.** TP4 Flash-Next, `flashnext-mtp2` plus
  `--monokernel`: K1, K2, GDN and QSA are on, K2 serves the 3- and 6-row FULL
  graphs, the W4A8 GEMM runs from 512 tokens, and the greedy, PPL, prefix-cache
  and concurrency probes pass before and after the load hook. Seven arms,
  no KFD eviction, no new AER line and no amdgpu fault in any of them.
- **Prefill is unchanged** (mono + W4A8 vs production): solo TTFT 4k 1.42 vs
  1.43 s (-0.6 %), 16k 5.62 vs 5.66 s (-0.7 %), 16k c=8 aggregate prefill 2,663
  vs 2,638 tok/s (+0.9 %), all inside the noise. The W4A8 win survives inside
  mono: against mono with W4A8 off (arm b), TTFT is -22 % at 4k and -23 % at
  16k and aggregate prefill is +26 %.
- **Quality equals production within noise.** Teacher-forced KL of mono + W4A8
  vs production is 0.01346 nats/token on docs and 0.02205 on mix, against
  boot-to-boot floors of 0.01393 and 0.02093 (production) and 0.01346 and 0.01859
  (mono + W4A8); the NLL shift is +0.00019 [-0.00162, +0.00201] and -0.00682
  [-0.02808, +0.01241]. The mechanical rule (KL <= floor + 0.003, NLL interval
  contains 0) passes on both domains. Decode-path logprob parity is inside the
  production boot-to-boot floor.
- **Decode: a small win over the recipe, no win over the queue budget.** ITL
  p50 at c=1 / 6 / 8, mono + W4A8 vs the shipped production recipe: -1.9 % /
  -3.2 % / -0.6 % (c=8 inside the noise). Production at 4 queues (arm e) alone
  gives -7.1 % / -5.5 % / -3.9 %, so mono + W4A8 is +5.6 % / +2.4 % / +3.4 %
  slower than production at an equal budget; at 2 queues (arm d) it is
  +2.1 % / +1.0 % / +1.8 % slower. The gain over the shipped recipe is the
  queue budget, not the kernels.
- **What to do with it.** Allowing both is safe and changes nothing when the
  flag is off, so the guard stays lifted. On this evidence `--monokernel` is
  not a decode upgrade for `flashnext-mtp2`; `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=4` in the
  production recipe is (see [What to do next](#what-to-do-next)).

All numbers are from tree `5b8d1e5570` on HIP 2-5, 2026-11-05 10:12-12:10 CET.
Raw outputs are in `bench_results/2026-11-05_mono-w4a8/` (`results/tables.md`
has every table; `results/results.json` the numbers behind them).

## What changed

The guard was policy, not a layout problem. W4A8 is the same MoE method on the
same packed weights, scales and zeros: weight load only sets a Python flag
(`_w4a8`), so K2 binds the tensors the W4A8 GEMM reads and nothing is repacked.
Decode stays W4A16 (K2 up to 8 rows, the modules for 9-24 rows); the W4A8 GEMM
runs from `W4A8_MOE_MIN_TOKENS` (512) tokens, in steps K1, K2, GDN and QSA never
serve.

- `vllm/models/qwen4_exp/amd/mono_decode_rdna2.py`: `moe_refusal` accepts the
  flag, the rank log line lists `W4A8 MoE prefill from 512 tokens`, and the
  expert cache (`expert_cache_slots`) refuses W4A8 at the API server and per
  layer (cached experts use the W4A16 fixed-point kernel only).
- `vllm/models/qwen4_exp/amd/mono/rdna2/layout.py`: the W4A8 binding.
- Tests: `tests/models/qwen4_exp/test_amd_mono_rdna2_w4a8.py` (CPU).
- Flag off and default are byte-identical to `rdna_extras`.
- The GPU plan that produced the numbers below is `tools/rdna/mono/w4a8/`
  (`slot.sh`, `arm_driver.py`, `analyze.py`, `queue_probe.py`,
  `gpu_temps.py`); see `tools/rdna/mono/README.md`.

Per arm: boot with the probes (greedy, PPL, prefix cache, concurrency), a load
hook (`tools/rdna/mono/w4a8/hook.sh`) with unmeasured warm-ups of each prompt

more as the same-boot repeat). Every cell sits between KFD eviction snapshots
(`tools/rdna/port_v031/kfd_evictions.py`); a window with a counter jump is
invalid and runs again. The queue budget cannot be read from a worker (vLLM
retitles its processes, which overwrites `/proc/<pid>/environ`), so
`queue_probe.py` lists the compute queues KFD gave each worker every 20 s, and
the engine's own raise line is checked as well.

## Things that went wrong

1. **A check that could never pass.** The slot checked "workers spawned with the
   arm's knobs" from `/proc/<pid>/environ`. vLLM retitles its workers
   (`setproctitle`), which overwrites that block, so the check failed with 0/4 on
   every arm and the slot wrote no `DONE` marker; the measurements were not
   affected. The check is replaced by the KFD compute queues (3 at a budget of 2,
   5 at 4: `budget + 1`, CLR's own queue on top) and the engine's raise line. The
   report was regenerated with the corrected checks; every arm passes
   (`arms/*/check-slot.txt` keeps the original output).
2. **Stray pollers of the author.** Three slot tests timed out on the loaded
   host and left their observers behind (`gpu_temps.py` at 5 Hz on HIP 2-5 and
   6-9, `queue_probe.py`), polling sysfs from 11:07 to 11:52, which covers arms
   a2, d and e. They were killed at 11:52:48 and the tests now run `slot.sh` in
   its own process group. Harmless for the numbers: arm e rebooted without them
   gave 1.417 / 5.659 s TTFT and 22.43 / 41.32 / 43.85 ms ITL against 1.427 /
   5.658 s and 22.47 / 41.16 / 44.23 ms with them.
3. **Evictions from other lanes (not in the seven arms).** That rerun of arm e
   (12:11-12:35) lost its 16k c=8 cell three times to KFD evictions of all four
   workers at once: up to +7.1 s, then +1.3 s, then +0.04 s per worker. The
   bursts coincide with the boots of TP2 servers of other lanes on other GPUs
   (weight loads at 12:23, 12:27 and 12:31); the page cache was full (487 GB
   cached, 3 GB free at 12:38). The eviction gate invalidated
   the cells instead of reporting them. The first pass (10:12-12:10) had none.
   A TP4 window on HIP 2-5 is valid only while nothing boots or loads weights
   elsewhere on the box. The rerun was stopped as redundant; its valid cells are
   in `arms-second-pass/`.

## Limits

- One boot per arm; a, a2 and c, c2 are the only repeats, and a cell has 4-32
  requests. Differences below about 1 % are not claimed.
- The teacher-forced windows score the prefill path. The decode path (the mono
  kernels) is covered by the greedy probes and the logprob parity, not by a KL
  over long windows, a GSM8K run or a 16-64k long-context KL (the gates in the
  [design](qwen4/monokernel-design.md)).
- The c arm has no KFD queue samples (the probe started after it); c2 has the
  same configuration, and the engine's raise line is in c's log.
- The 4-queue production arm is one recipe change measured on this tree. The
  [2026-11-02 ABBA](async-stall-profile-2026-11-02.md) found 4 queues slower in
  TTFT (20.0 vs 17.7 s) and faster in decode; the TTFT cost does not reproduce
  here (16k c=8 mean 18.98 vs 18.80 and 19.38 s at 2), but that needs its own
  ABBA before a recipe changes.

## What to do next

1. Merge the guard lift: both flags together are correct, covered by tests, and
   the default is untouched.
2. Do not add `--monokernel` to `flashnext-mtp2` for decode. At an equal queue
   budget mono + W4A8 is 1-6 % slower on ITL p50 than the stock decode graphs
   on this tree (c=6 at 2 queues is within the noise).
3. Run an ABBA of `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES` 4 vs 2 for `flashnext-mtp2` with W4A8
   prefill (this run: ITL p50 -7.1 % / -5.5 % / -3.9 %, no TTFT cost), including
   the rdna_ar stress that motivated the 2. **Done 2026-11-06:**
   [hwq-abba-2026-11-06.md](hwq-abba-2026-11-06.md) confirms it (median ITL
   -6.6 / -4.5 / -4.2 %) and the recipe is at 4 now.
4. Schedule TP4 windows with the other lanes: nothing may boot or load weights
   on the other GPUs during a measured window.

## Reproduce

```bash
EXPECT_SHA=<sha> ARMS="c a c2 a2 d e b" RUN=~/w4a8_runs/mono-w4a8 setsid nohup \
    bash tools/rdna/mono/w4a8/slot.sh > $RUN/slot.log 2>&1 < /dev/null &
touch $RUN/GO
```

`slot.sh` takes the lease, boots each arm through
`tools/rdna/port_v031/serve_validate.sh`, writes `results/tables.md` and
releases the lease; the details are in `tools/rdna/mono/README.md`.
