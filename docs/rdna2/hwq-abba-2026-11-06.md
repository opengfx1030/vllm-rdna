# `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES` 4 vs 2 for `flashnext-mtp2`: ABBA (2026-11-06)

Decision: `flashnext-mtp2` runs at `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=4` from this commit on.
The four other Flash-Next recipes were already at 4; every recipe that omits
the key still gets the launcher's 2.

## Why a new ABBA

The recipe sat at 2 because of a 2026-11-02 ABBA (16k/1k c=8 TTFT mean 20.0 ->
17.7 s, decode -3 to -6 %). The unpaired production control in
[mono-w4a8-2026-11-05.md](mono-w4a8-2026-11-05.md) (arm `e`, one boot at 4
against two boots at 2) contradicted it: ITL p50 -7.1 / -5.5 / -3.9 % at
c=1 / 6 / 8 with TTFT unchanged. This run settles it with a paired design.

## Method

- Four fresh boots of `flashnext-mtp2` (TP4 / EP4, MTP 2, AWQ-W4A16) in the
  order A1, B1, B2, A2 (A = 2 queues, B = 4), on HIP 6,7,8,9 (V620 buses
  `0a`/`0f`/`14`/`17`, resolved and verified before every boot). Exact-SHA tree
  `c6b1ff354e` (`rdna_extras` tip: the mlock merge), `.so` set from the shared
  build dir after `git diff 47c426fb43 c6b1ff354e -- csrc cmake CMakeLists.txt
  setup.py rust` came back empty. A cold `VLLM_CACHE_ROOT` per boot.
- The only difference between boots is the `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=2|4` override on
  the `serve_validate.sh` command line. `serve_rdna.sh PRINT=1` of every boot
  exports the wanted value, and KFD lists 3 compute queues per worker at 2 and
  5 at 4, before and after the cells (a worker's `/proc/<pid>/environ` is
  overwritten by `setproctitle` and cannot show it).
- Per boot: probe, PPL probe, warm-up, concurrency probe, then the cells, then
  the probe again. Cells: 1k/512 at c=1 / 6 / 8 and 16k/1024 at c=1 / 8, random
  prompts, `--ignore-eos`, `--temperature 0`, seeds 12301..12305 identical in
  all four boots, each cell inside a KFD `evicted_ms` window and a quiet
  window of the lease protocol.
- The decision rule was fixed beforehand: ship 4 only if the median ITL
  improves by more than the within-condition spread (the larger of
  |A1-A2|, |B1-B2|) at c=1 and c=8, TTFT and the prefill aggregate are not
  worse beyond spread, and probes, PPL and the concurrency probe pass.

## Result

All 20 cells had 0 ms KFD eviction on all four workers (and 0 ms over each whole
run), so no cell was rerun. Junction temperature peaked at 79-81 C.

ITL median (ms); B vs A compares the mean of A1/A2 with the mean of B1/B2:

| cell | A1 | A2 | B1 | B2 | mean A | mean B | B vs A | spread A / B |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1k c1 | 23.75 | 24.78 | 22.62 | 22.69 | 24.26 | 22.66 | **-6.6 %** | 1.03 / 0.06 |
| 1k c6 | 42.87 | 44.46 | 41.38 | 42.01 | 43.67 | 41.69 | **-4.5 %** | 1.59 / 0.63 |
| 1k c8 | 45.67 | 47.26 | 44.52 | 44.45 | 46.46 | 44.49 | **-4.2 %** | 1.59 / 0.07 |
| 16k c1 | 24.43 | 25.32 | 23.22 | 23.07 | 24.88 | 23.15 | **-6.9 %** | 0.88 / 0.15 |
| 16k c8 | 48.07 | 49.60 | 46.68 | 46.37 | 48.84 | 46.53 | **-4.7 %** | 1.53 / 0.31 |

Every B boot is faster than both A boots in every cell, so the A1 -> A2 drift
(about 1 ms slower) does not explain the gap.

- TTFT median and p99, and the prefill aggregate (one wave of prompts over p99
  TTFT): within spread in every cell (1k: TTFT median -0.1 to -1.4 %, prefill
  aggregate +0.3 to +0.8 %; 16k c1: -0.5 % and +0.5 %). At 16k c8 the prefill
  aggregate is +1.7 % and the p99 TTFT -1.7 %, both slightly beyond spread in
  B's favour. The 16k c8 TTFT median (15.7 s in A1, 7.4-8.6 s in the other
  three) is a queueing artifact of 24 requests behind the first wave and is not
  a decision metric.
- Mean ITL: -2.0 to -3.6 %, beyond spread at c=1 only; at c=6 / 8 and 16k c8 the
  mean carries the prefill-interference tail (ITL p99 within spread).
- Output tok/s and MTP acceptance length move with the sampled tokens, not with
  the step rate (1k c1 acceptance: A1 1.50, A2 1.94, B1 2.07, B2 2.12). At 16k
  c8 B's acceptance is lower (2.27 against 2.40, beyond spread) while output
  tok/s is unchanged (+0.4 %); flagged, not a gate metric.
- Probe, PPL probe (4.81 / 4.83 / 4.82 / 4.86 for A1 / B1 / B2 / A2),
  concurrency probe and the probe after load: PASS in all four boots.
- dmesg: no `Runlist is getting oversubscribed` line, and no new amdgpu fault,
  timeout, reset or AER line, in any of the four boots.

The unpaired 2026-11-05 control (-7.1 / -5.5 / -3.9 % at c=1 / 6 / 8) is
reproduced (-6.6 / -4.5 / -4.2 %). The 2026-11-02 ABBA's 16k c=8 TTFT mean
advantage for 2 is not: the TTFT medians are within spread here and the 16k c8
prefill aggregate is not lower at 4.

## Scope and caveats

- `--monokernel` still raises the workers to 4 by itself; with the recipe at 4
  that raise is a no-op. The `tools/rdna/mono/w4a8/slot.sh` arms `a` and `d`
  that assumed the recipe's 2 now run at 4 (arm `a` equals arm `e`).
- Slot B only; one model and one prompt family (random tokens). The rdna_ar
  stress that originally motivated 2 was not rerun; the concurrency probe and
  the 16k c8 cell (where the one-shot all-reduce is active on every rank) were
  clean.
- The bench client ran from `venv-7.14.0_0.32.0`, the same in all four boots.

Raw data and scripts: `bench_results/2026-11-06_hwq-abba/` (`report.md` has the
full per-boot, per-cell tables).
