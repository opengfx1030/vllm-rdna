| Part | Bytes | Per rank at TP2 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| hi2 body (496 linears, 48 GDN + 16 full-attn layers) | 9.169 GB (8.54 GiB) | ~4.58 GB |
| `lm_head` fp16 | 2.543 GB | 1.271 GB |
| dense body leftovers (norms, conv1d, A_log...) | 0.005 GB | replicated |
| **target step** | **11.72 GB** | **~5.86 GB** |
| MTP layer (dense bf16 -> fp16) | 0.849 GB | 0.425 GB |
| **one draft step** (MTP layer + shared `lm_head`) | **3.39 GB (0.29 x target)** | **1.70 GB** |

`embed_tokens` (2.543 GB) is a row lookup, not a full read. Per-rank hi2 bytes
assume the row/column split of `RdnaHintFlexLinearMethod._partitions` is even
[inference]. Quantizing the MTP layer to hi2 would save at most 0.69 GB [inference: 0.849 GB to about 0.16 GB at 3 bpw]
(0.34 GB/rank, 20 % of a draft step) and the quantizer does not do it today;
shrinking the draft `lm_head` read (vocab restriction) would pay more but is a
new feature, and `draft_lm_head_quantization` is FP8/NVFP4 only.

## Does MTP pay on hi2? [inference]

Not from this work's data. Cost model from the published hi2 decode kernel
table (`rdna-hint-flex-weights.md`, "Kernel timing (decode)": the GEMV re-reads
the compact weights once per 8 tokens, so time grows with M: gate_up 40.1 us at
M=1, 71.5 at M=3, 138.5 at M=8, 535.9 at M=32; down 28.1/41.5/74.8/294.3),
`lm_head` and the drafter bandwidth-bound and M-independent for M <= 24, draft
step = 0.29 of a target step, no fixed per-step cost (all-reduce latency, GDN,
attention are **not** modelled and would all make MTP look better). Speedup =
`E(k,p) x step(0) / step(k)` with `E = sum_{i<=k} p^i`, one acceptance
probability `p` per draft token:

| c | MTP k | p=0.6 | p=0.7 | p=0.8 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 1 | 1.00-1.08 | 1.07-1.15 | 1.13-1.22 |
| 1 | 2 | 0.89-1.00 | 1.00-1.12 | 1.11-1.25 |
| 1 | 3 | 0.79-0.90 | 0.92-1.04 | 1.08-1.22 |
| 8 | 1 | 0.80-0.81 | 0.85-0.86 | 0.89-0.91 |
| 8 | 2 | 0.65-0.66 | 0.72-0.74 | 0.81-0.82 |
| 8 | 3 | 0.54-0.55 | 0.63-0.64 | 0.73-0.75 |

(ranges: the `down` shape's scaling, then `gate_up`'s). Reading: on hi2 the
verify batch is not free (unlike a bandwidth-bound AWQ decode), so MTP helps at
c=1-2, is about neutral for k=2 near p=0.7 and **loses at c=8**; the verify
rows `c x (k+1)` also grow the GEMV cost linearly. The only acceptance data in
the repo for this model family is EXL3 27B TP4 MTP2: mean acceptance length
2.68-2.84 eager (`bench_results/2026-10-01_exl3-27b-mtp2/SUMMARY.md`; different
quantization, so hi2 at 3 bpw may differ: the MTP layer was trained on the
unquantized hidden states). Hence the recipe below ships `MTP=2` as the
latency setting and the GPU A/B decides the default.

## Recipe fragment for `27b-hi2-tp2` (do not create the file here)

Follows `27b-awq.env` / `flashnext-mtp2.env`; `HiTp2Bring` owns the base recipe.

```text
# --- MTP (dense mtp.* layer; docs/rdna2/hi2-27b-mtp-2026-11-14.md) ---
# Launcher expands MTP=2 to
#   --speculative-config '{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":2,"use_local_argmax_reduction":true}'
# and the capture ladder [3,6,12,24] (CG_CAPTURE_SIZES=1, CG_MODE=FULL_AND_PIECEWISE).
# Equivalent explicit form (MTP must still be set for the ladder):
#   SPEC_CONFIG='{"method":"mtp","num_speculative_tokens":2,"use_local_argmax_reduction":true}'
MTP=2
SEQS=8                         # verify rows 3 x 8 = 24, within hi2 DECODE_MAX_M 48 (GEMV path)
# MTP2 verify batches at hidden 5120: all-reduce 24 x 5120 x 2 = 240 KiB, mtp.fc
# gather 24 x 2560 x 2 = 120 KiB; the 27b-awq gate of 64 KiB sends both to RCCL.
VLLM_RDNA_AR_MAX_KB=256
VLLM_RDNA_AR_ONESHOT_KB=256
# Everything else as 27b-awq.env (conv1d HIP kernels off, FULL_AND_PIECEWISE,
# FEATURES with mamba-align). TP=2.
```

Throughput recipes at c >= 4 should keep `MTP=0` until the A/B below says
otherwise.
