## Verdicts

| question | answer | evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why is the c=8 input rate 35 % below c=1? | 63 % of the gap is decode time (121 s of decode-only steps that a prefill rate does not contain); 32 % is the mixed steps: each carries 1024 prompt tokens plus the 7 decode rows through one GEMM of M = 1031, which costs 27 % more per token than M = 2048 (tile padding, no tuned rocBLAS solution); 5 % is the decode rows' own work. | section 1, section 2.3 |

| All-reduce fix (>= 8 % rule) | Not built as a default: 7.8 % is below the bar, RCCL already moves 20 MiB at 12.8 GB/s (the card's PCIe ceiling is about 14 GB/s `[INFERENCE]`), and GEMM/all-reduce overlap measured no gain. | section 3 |

## Method

* `tools/rdna/rdna_hint_flex/c8_profile.py` boots the recipe under `rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --selected-regions`
  (the roctx shim; SIGUSR1/SIGUSR2 open and close a window), logs every engine step's composition and wall time
  (`--enable-logging-iteration-details`), drives the `c8` workload (the scoreboard cell: 16 random 16384/1024
  requests, 8 concurrent) and opens a window per phase read from that log: `prefill` (first request alone),
  `mixed` (7 decoders + 1 prefilling request), `decode` (8 decoders). Both TP ranks are traced.
* `c8_breakdown.py` cuts each window into model steps at the lm_head GEMV (one per step), types every step from its
  kernels, and sums kernel time per family and roll-up category, plus the GPU idle time in front of each launch.
  The final recipe prefills with `VLLM_FA_RDNA2_UNFUSED_PREFILL=1`, so a chunk has no `fa_prefill` kernel: a chunk is
  recognised by its GDN prefill kernels, and its two attention GEMMs (S = QK^T and O = PV, rocBLAS) are told from the
  linear-layer GEMMs by their position around the softmax.
* `c8_timeline.py` rebuilds the scheduler timeline of a `vllm bench serve` cell from its JSON alone.
* `bench_allreduce.py` (RCCL vs `rdna_ar` sweep, GEMM + all-reduce overlap) and `c8_session.sh` (one GPU session
  between two scoreboard arms) are the session tools. `rocprofv3` does not attach to the venv runtime for counters;
  these are kernel traces.
* Overhead of the profiler: a decode step is 67.7 ms in the trace against 63.3 ms in the bench (+7 %); a mixed step
  is 1669 ms against 1672 ms (none).

## 1. The scheduler timeline of the 16k/1k c=8 cell

`c8_timeline.py` on `bench-16384x1024-c8.json` (`final`, HIP 2,3) and on the same cell measured on slot a2
(`final-a2`, HIP 0,1), 16 requests of 16384 prompt tokens:

| | final (HIP 2,3) | final-a2 (HIP 0,1) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| cell duration | 556.9 s | 540.7 s |
| c=1 prefill rate (same arm) | 720 tok/s | 751 tok/s |
| idle-engine prefill time (262144 tokens at that rate) | 364.1 s | 349.1 s |
| duration above it | 192.8 s (35 %) | 191.6 s (35 %) |
| pure prefill steps (2048 tokens) | 10 steps, 26.2 s | 10 steps, 25.5 s |
| mixed steps (1024 prompt tokens + 1..7 decode rows) | 236 steps, 409.8 s, mean 1734.8 ms (p99 1842.9) | 236 steps, 394.8 s, mean 1671.5 ms |
| decode-only steps, 8 decoders | 1827 steps, 116.0 s, 63.6 ms | 1827 steps, 115.5 s, 63.3 ms |
| decode-only steps, 1..7 decoders (ramp and tail) | 115 steps, 5.0 s | 115 steps, 5.0 s |
| prefill-bearing phases | 436.0 s = 601 tok/s | 420.3 s = 624 tok/s |

236 x 1024 + 10 x 2048 = 262144: every prompt token is accounted for, and every mixed step carries **1024**
prompt tokens, not 2048. The profile's iteration log shows why: all 236 mixed steps are `1 context request, 1024
context tokens, 1..7 generation tokens`. `serve_rdna.sh` sets the budget to two mamba blocks (2 x 1024 = 2048) so
that two blocks are prefilled per step, but the 7 decode rows take 7 of the 2048 tokens, and the hybrid scheduler
(`Scheduler._mamba_block_aligned_split`) rounds the remaining 2041 down to a block multiple: 1024.

What the 192.8 s above the idle-engine time is made of (`final`):

| component | seconds | share of the gap | what it is |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| decode-only phases | 121.0 | 62.8 % | 16 x 1024 output tokens have to be generated; at c=1 the same decode time exists but a prefill tok/s does not contain it. Not a prefill inefficiency. |
| prefill-bearing phases above 720 tok/s | 71.9 | 37.2 % | 436.0 s against 364.1 s |
| of which: GEMM of M = 1031 against M = 2048 per token | about 61 | 31.6 % | +256 ms (rank 0) / +267 ms (rank 1) per mixed step x 236 (section 2.3) |
| of which: the decode rows' own work and per-step costs | about 11 | 5.6 % | decode attention 23-24 ms per mixed step (5.5 s) and the rest |

The 10 % "ramp/tail/interleave overhead" of the first estimate (about 63 s) is the second line: the cell is
within 13 % of `364 s of prefill + 130 s of decode`, and the part of that 13 % that is not decode rows is the M = 1031
GEMM.

### 2.3 The mixed step against half a pure step

A mixed step does half the prompt tokens of a pure step. Kernel time against half a pure step (rank 0 / rank 1, ms):

| family | half a pure step | mixed step | excess |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| linear-layer GEMMs | 952.8 / 979.4 | 1215.5 / 1253.1 | **+262.7 / +273.7** |
| decode attention | 0 | 23.1 / 24.0 | +23.1 / +24.0 |
| HI2 dequant (once per step, whatever the token count) | 23.0 / 23.5 | 41.8 / 42.3 | +18.8 / +18.8 |
| RCCL all-reduce | 137.7 / 105.7 | 152.8 / 107.9 | +15.0 / +2.2 |
| attention GEMMs | 109.0 / 111.8 | 95.6 / 98.6 | -13.5 / -13.2 |
| everything else | | | about +10 |
| **wall** | 1348.0 | 1668.7 | **+320.7** |

The GEMM excess is 82-85 % of the excess. Per token the linear GEMMs cost 0.930 ms at M = 2048 and 1.179 ms at M =
1031: 25.1 TFLOP in 1.22-1.25 s = **20.7 / 20.0 TF/s**, 21-22 % below the pure step. Two causes, both visible in the
trace: (1) the token dimension is tiled by 256 (`MT128x256x16`) or 128 (`MT128x128x16`): 2048 = 8 x 256 fills every
tile, 1031 = 8 x 128 + 7 pads to 1152 (89.5 % filled) or to 1280 (80.5 %); (2) the TunableOp rows of the recipe cover
M 1023/1024/2046/2047/2048 (`tunableop/rocm7.14-rocblas5.5-hi2-27b-tp2/README.md`), and at M = 1025..1031 its tuner
found only the `Default` solver or one under 3 % faster: no rocBLAS solution avoids the extra tile row. rocBLAS therefore
picks per GEMM between the two: the mixed step runs 128 launches of `MT128x128x16` (5.09 ms mean) and 128 of
`MT128x256x16` (4.37 ms), the pure step runs only `MT128x256x16`. The HIP dense GEMM op has a tail split for exactly
this case (`RDNA_HINT_FLEX_PREFILL_GEMM=hip`, mode 4: the 7 tail rows go to the decode GEMV, the 1024 whole rows to the
dense kernel; `docs/rdna2/hi2-prefill-gemm2-2026-11-15.md`), see section 2.5.

Per step fixed costs matter too: the dequant (46 ms), the lm_head GEMV (3 ms), 3300 launches and the all-reduce
latencies are paid per step, so a step of 1024 tokens pays them twice per 2048 tokens.

### 3.2 The exposed share of a chunk

Per 2048-token chunk (rank 0 / rank 1): 129 all-reduces, 275.5 / 211.4 ms, 10.2 % / 7.8 % of 2696 ms. Pairing the
launches of the two ranks (`c8.md`, last table) splits that: the data time (the shorter of the two durations of a
pair) is 210.9 ms per chunk, and the sum of the start skews is 66.3 ms: rank 0 arrives early at every all-reduce and
waits, because its GEMMs run 53 ms faster per chunk than rank 1's (1905.6 against 1958.8 ms): both cards sit at the
175 W cap in the prefill window (junction 69 C), and rank 0's shader clock averages 1856 MHz against 1795 MHz on rank 1
(`thermal.csv`, 3.3 % apart, GEMMs 2.8 % apart). The critical path
carries 211 ms = **7.8 %**; the earlier 10.7 % of `hi2-27b-tp2-2026-11-14.md` was rank 0's number with the skew
inside. Mixed steps: 107.9 ms of data time on a 1668.7 ms step = 6.5 %.

## 4. Decode: what the step is made of and what the roofline says (8 decoders x 16.5k, per rank)

Bytes per step per rank: weights 4.56 GB at 3 bits (gate_up 64 x 33.4 MB, down 64 x 16.7 MB, GDN qkvz 48 x 15.7 MB,
GDN out and attention o 64 x 5.9 MB, attention qkv 16 x 13.8 MB; scales not counted), lm_head 1.27 GB (fp16, half the
vocabulary), KV cache 4.3 GB (8 rows x 16.5k tokens x 2 KV heads x 256 x 2 B x 2 x 16 layers). 10.1 GB: 22.4 ms at
450 GB/s achievable `[INFERENCE: the card's 512 GB/s peak is not reached by any kernel measured here]`.

| slice | measured | byte floor at 450 GB/s | gap | achieved |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| decode attention | 32.3 ms | 9.6 ms | 22.7 ms | 134 GB/s (2.02 ms per layer) |
| HI2 GEMV | 18.2 ms (gemv2 17.7 ms) | 10.1 ms | 7.6 ms | 257 GB/s |
| lm_head GEMV | 3.0 ms | 2.8 ms | 0.2 ms | 423 GB/s |
| all-reduce, GDN, norms, glue | 7.5 ms | | | |
| GPU idle gaps | 6.1 ms | | | |

HI2 GEMV by shape class (grid from the trace; per rank, launches per step; bytes at 3 bits per weight):

| class (N x K) | launches | mean us | MB | GB/s |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| gate_up 17408 x 5120 | 64 | 117.4 | 33.4 | 285 |
| GDN qkvz 8192 x 5120 | 48 | 61.4 | 15.7 | 256 |
| attention qkv 7168 x 5120 | 16 | 55.9 | 13.8 | 246 |
| down 5120 x 8704 and out/o 5120 x 3072 (one grid) | 64 + 64 | 49.7 | 16.7 and 5.9 | 227 (pair average) |

The N = 5120 pair is the least efficient (6.4 ms per step, 227 GB/s) and the smallest-K kernels dominate the gap.
Reaching 285 GB/s everywhere would save 1.7 ms per step (2.5 %); reaching 400 GB/s would save 6.3 ms (9 %) but is a
new kernel (the v2 kernel is VGPR 240 and likely ALU-bound at 8 rows `[INFERENCE]`): not attempted here.

The decode attention is the only slice that clears the 1.5 % bar with a plausible kernel-level win, so item 3 is the
attention kernel (section 5).

## 5. What is wired (every switch default off or default-unchanged)

| switch | default | what it does | GPU status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `VLLM_FA_RDNA2_GQA_DECODE=2` | the launcher sets 1 for `ATTN=fa`; 2 only when passed as `KEY=VAL` | `fa_decode_paged_splitk_gqa_vec_kernel_256` in `csrc/rocm/fa_rdna2.cu`: same grid, split layout, partials and per-element arithmetic order as mode 1 (bit-identical by construction), 16-byte LDS traffic: K rows read as `uint4` from a 264-half row stride (conflict-free), V staged transposed (`sVt[d][token]`, one 16-byte store per 8 tokens, one 16-byte read per 8 tokens of a thread's PV column shared by all 6 heads), P read as `float4`. About 120 LDS instructions per thread and 32-token tile against about 700. | compiled and linked on the box (`_rocm_C` md5 `4b4b92b4`, same 180 torch op schemas as the old one); never loaded: the venv's `sitecustomize` imports `vllm._rocm_C` at interpreter start, so a second copy cannot be loaded beside it and the first load is `install-so` + `attn-check` in session B; **not yet run on a GPU** |

Tests: `tests/kernels/attention/test_fa_rdna2_gqa_decode_vec.py` (runs `tools/rdna/port_v031/gqa_decode_check.py` once per mode and compares the output hashes; skips off gfx103x), `tests/distributed/test_rdna_ar_vpush_gpu.py`
(allowlisted in `tools/pre_commit/test_tethering_allowlist.txt`), `tests/tools/rdna/test_c8_profile_dry_run.py` (CPU: the profile driver against a fake server and a stub rocprofv3; 2 passed on the box).

What the attention kernel is worth, from the profile and the roofline (`[INFERENCE]` until the GPU check): the old kernel costs
2.02 ms per layer for 268 MB of KV (134 GB/s). At 300-420 GB/s it costs 0.9-0.64 ms per layer, 17.9-22 ms per decode
step (a 63.5 ms ITL becomes about 42-46 ms), and over the 1827 decode-only steps of the cell 33-40 s (6-7 % of 557 s).
Why the LDS is the target `[INFERENCE]`: with `kv_splits` 16 the grid is 8 x 2 x 16 = 256 CTAs; if 3 CTAs fit a WGP's LDS (39 KB each)
they run in three rounds of 32 tiles, about 21 us per 32-token tile and CTA, which is what about 700 LDS instructions per thread
with bank conflicts and 8 waves per CTA cost on one CU. Not measured: no hardware counters were collected (the traces are kernel traces).
